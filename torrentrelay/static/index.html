How do I send from the share menu?

Open this page in Chrome on your phone, then menu → Add to Home screen / Install. After that, “Send to qBittorrent” shows up in the Share sheet — tap Share on any magnet link and choose it.

On iPhone, Safari doesn’t support share targets, so use the paste box above (or add a Shortcut that POSTs to /api/add).
